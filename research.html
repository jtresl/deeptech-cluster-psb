Research Areas

Two tracks, treated as one continuous problem space: following problems from their technical origins through to their managerial and economic consequences.

Track 1 · Computational Intelligence

The technological substrate of the modern economy. Working groups on the economic and strategic implications of frontier AI, the infrastructure and sustainability dimensions of data centre expansion, and quantum optimization for complex decision problems.

Artificial intelligenceData centresQuantum optimization

Track 2 · Business Intelligence

The managerial and economic layer where technology meets real-world constraints. Working groups on resilient supply chains in a fragmented geopolitical environment, sustainability in investment and wealth management, and green solutions for long-term value creation.

Long-term sustainabilityGreen solutionsSupply chainsWealth management

At the intersection: quantum computing for finance & business

How AI reshapes capital allocation, how data centres factor into sustainability strategies, how quantum optimization transforms supply chain resilience. See e.g. Ciceri et al. (2025), arXiv:2509.17715.
