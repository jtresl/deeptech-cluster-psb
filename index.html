AI · Cryptography

Claude reveals ECDSA curve origins

Anthropic · 16 Oct 2026

An Anthropic model traced the NSA-supplied seeds of the NIST P-curves to a 1990s lrand48 random-number generator seeded with 1997–98 Unix timestamps, solving a 25-year mystery in under half an hour.

Cluster Comment“

The real signal is that AI did in under 30 minutes what humans failed at for 25 years, so legacy cryptography in payments now deserves AI-assisted audits.

AI · Finance

Anthropic's junk credit rating went unreported during $35 billion AI financing

The Deep Dive · 16 Oct 2026

KBRA rated Anthropic BB+, one notch below investment grade, before the Apollo-led $35bn chip financing; the $4.4bn junior tranche sold at about 8.5% versus 5.75% for Broadcom-guaranteed senior debt.

Cluster Comment“

The 275-basis-point gap prices Anthropic as a high-yield credit, yet its obligations sit in SPVs off its own balance sheet, leaving AI-lab leverage largely invisible to equity investors.

Data centres

AI datacenter capex will far exceed forecast revenue through 2030

Computerworld · 16 Oct 2026

Gartner estimates data-centre owners need $8.5 trillion in revenue through 2030 to cover capex, financing and returns, against at most $3.6 trillion of attributable AI revenue.

Cluster Comment“

Hyperscalers can absorb the gap but neoclouds cannot, so the first losses will likely surface as neocloud consolidation and lender write-downs rather than in Big Tech earnings.

Supply chain

Taiwan to impose strict controls on AI chip exports to China

Taiwan News · 16 Oct 2026

Taiwan will add advanced semiconductors and AI servers to its export control list, making unauthorised shipments to China a criminal offence under the Foreign Trade Act, in line with its US trade deal.

Cluster Comment“

Smugglers so far faced only forgery and breach-of-trust charges, so criminal liability shifts compliance risk onto server integrators and resellers, though no effective date has been set.

Quantum

A path toward larger quantum processors

RIKEN · 16 Oct 2026

RIKEN researchers drove five silicon spin qubits in parallel through one shared microwave line, using pairwise calibration to cancel phase shifts and keep fidelity near 99.9% (Nature Physics).

Cluster Comment“

Fidelity slipped from above 99.99% with three qubits to about 99.9% with five, so shared control lines may scale only until drive-induced decoherence, not calibration, becomes the binding constraint.

Quantum

D-Wave delivers Italy's first annealing quantum computer

D-Wave · 15 Oct 2026

A 4,400+ qubit Advantage2 system, called VoltaB1, arrived in Como under a €10M deal in which Swiss Quantum Technology took half its capacity for five years, with an option to buy the system.

Cluster Comment“

The deal structure is the story: buying 50% of capacity for five years with a purchase option turns a quantum computer into a leased infrastructure asset that financiers can underwrite.

Deep Tech · Funding

Nauta secures €60 million first close for sixth fund to back European software and DeepTech startups

EU-Startups · 15 Oct 2026

The London-based VC, anchored by €30M from the British Business Bank, targets €150–200M to back pre-seed to Series A B2B software and deep tech, including AI infrastructure and HPC.

Cluster Comment“

With half the first close from a state bank and most late-stage capital still non-European, Europe's deep tech pipeline is publicly seeded but likely to be exited abroad.

Quantum

IonQ and the University of Cambridge open the IonQ Quantum Innovation Centre

IonQ · 15 Oct 2026

The centre at the Cavendish Laboratory's Ray Dolby Centre will host a 256-qubit Superion system and an on-campus quantum network, with nine initial projects including quantum portfolio optimisation.

Cluster Comment“

Because the IP behind new network nodes feeds straight into IonQ's roadmap, the UK supplies the research talent while much of the commercial value may accrue to a US-listed firm.
