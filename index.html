Our research tracksMeet the team

About & Mission

DeepTech brings together academics from various backgrounds and schools with the goal to generate wealth that will ultimately benefit PSB and our partners. Given the volatile geopolitical environment, our focus addresses problems that significantly affect the global economy.

A space and a community

Both a physical space and an intellectual community, where researchers, doctoral students, and industry partners converge to exchange ideas, co-develop projects, and turn cross-disciplinary conversations into concrete outputs.

Why it is different

A deliberate pairing of two tracks rarely combined in a business school: the technical frontier of AI, data infrastructure and quantum, and the managerial frontier of sustainability, supply chains and wealth management.

Destination 2030

Aligned with PSB's Destination 2030 strategic plan and its two core pillars: transdisciplinarity and internationalization. The cluster will grow from six founding internal members to at least eight PSB members by the end of Year 2.

Research Areas

Two tracks, treated as one continuous problem space: following problems from their technical origins through to their managerial and economic consequences.

Track 1 · Computational Intelligence

The technological substrate of the modern economy. Working groups on the economic and strategic implications of frontier AI, the infrastructure and sustainability dimensions of data centre expansion, and quantum optimization for complex decision problems.

Artificial intelligenceData centresQuantum optimization

Track 2 · Business Intelligence

The managerial and economic layer where technology meets real-world constraints. Working groups on resilient supply chains in a fragmented geopolitical environment, sustainability in investment and wealth management, and green solutions for long-term value creation.

Long-term sustainabilityGreen solutionsSupply chainsWealth management

At the intersection: quantum computing for finance & business

How AI reshapes capital allocation, how data centres factor into sustainability strategies, how quantum optimization transforms supply chain resilience. See e.g. Ciceri et al. (2025), arXiv:2509.17715.

Activities & Outputs

Research output

Peer-reviewed publications in leading journals across both tracks, with emphasis on joint papers across departments and with external partners.

Events

At least one annual workshop or conference (the annual DeepTech convening, co-organised with a rotating international partner), plus regular internal seminars and writing sessions.

External funding

A pipeline of proposals (Horizon Europe, ERC, ANR, France 2030 / Bpifrance, EIC, industry chairs, foundations), with at least one major proposal per year.

Knowledge mobilization

Engagement with policy makers, industry leaders, NGOs, and media so findings reach audiences beyond academia; quarterly practitioner briefing.

Teaching impact

Research-based teaching content for PSB programs, with particular benefit to the DBA.

Training

Co-supervision of doctoral and DBA projects, a doctoral methods school, masterclasses on AI methods, quantum optimization and sustainability metrics, and mentorship pairings.
