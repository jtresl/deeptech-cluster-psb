AI banking
AI · Finance

Citi takes cautious approach on AI agent access

Banking Dive · 23 Oct 2026

Citi has blocked agentic access beyond read-only open banking and wants a “know your agent” process, as JPMorgan, Bank of America, Morgan Stanley and Schwab also block customers' external AI agents.

Cluster Comment“

By keeping agents read-only, banks preserve their control of the payment rail, so agentic commerce will likely run through bank-approved channels rather than third-party assistants that could disintermediate them.

Quantum

Fast but accurate: a new speed record for quantum gates

Forschungszentrum Jülich · 23 Oct 2026

Researchers from Jülich, Aachen and Cologne ran single-qubit gates in 6.8 nanoseconds with over 99.98% fidelity by suppressing multi-photon transitions, a result published in Nature Communications.

Cluster Comment“

Faster gates mean less idle decoherence per operation, but the result is from one isolated transmon, so the open question is whether two-qubit gates and crosstalk hold up at this speed.

Supply chain

Chip stocks lose $200 billion as equipment maker's warning counters NVIDIA's AI optimism

Tech Times · 23 Oct 2026

Lam Research posted record revenue but said supply-chain constraints trimmed its outlook into 2027, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 6.72% on 22 October.

Cluster Comment“

A $300–400 million shipment delay erased far more market value than it cost Lam, showing investors now price chip stocks on bottleneck risk rather than demand risk.

Quantum hardware
Quantum

IBM links two 120-qubit Nighthawk chips with four cables

Quantum Brief · 23 Oct 2026

IBM Research connected two 120-qubit processors with four 80 cm coaxial cables, moving quantum states across at 99.00% mean fidelity, and found that the magnetic field during cooldown can degrade a cable sixfold.

Cluster Comment“

Cross-chip entangling gates at 94.75% still trail on-chip gates by a wide margin, so modular scaling will initially cost error-correction overhead rather than save it.

Data centres · Finance

Nebius gets $3.5 billion Citi facility for data center growth

Reuters · 22 Oct 2026

The Amsterdam-based AI cloud firm took a 364-day senior secured facility at Term SOFR plus 2.75% to fund data centres and GPUs, a week after CoreWeave's bonds sold off sharply.

Cluster Comment“

Funding long-lived data centres with a 364-day loan creates refinancing risk next year, even though Nebius says it can also borrow against more than $40 billion of contracts.

Data centres · Energy

Neuberger Specialty Finance and Crusoe announce $700 million facility for AI factory power

Crusoe · 22 Oct 2026

The facility, secured by recently ordered gas generators, funds behind-the-meter power for Crusoe-built and -owned AI data centres, following Crusoe's $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion valuation.

Cluster Comment“

Lending against generators rather than the data centre gives creditors a movable, resaleable asset, which makes on-site gas power easier to finance than the AI capacity it serves.

AI · Finance

AI credit risk grows as debt finances the infrastructure build-out

CNBC · 22 Oct 2026

TS Lombard says debt is now the marginal source of AI funding, with about $700 billion of AI bond issuance expected in 2027, more than all euro-area governments combined.

Cluster Comment“

Once credit markets fund the marginal data centre, a Fed hike or a spread widening outside tech can halt the build-out even while AI demand stays strong.

Deep Tech · Funding

Dutch photonics startup Photon Bridge raises €10M to tackle AI's looming laser bottleneck

Tech.eu · 21 Oct 2026

The Eindhoven company combines many lower-power lasers instead of a few powerful ones to raise manufacturing yield, with first samples due in 2027 and volume production from 2028.

Cluster Comment“

Designing for production across multiple indium phosphide foundries is the real moat, because laser supply for co-packaged optics depends on yield and second sourcing, not peak performance.
