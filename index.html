Welcome

DeepTech is a transdisciplinary research cluster at Paris School of Business, led by Jiri Tresl, uniting two tracks: Computational Intelligence (AI, data centres, quantum optimization) and Business Intelligence (sustainability, green solutions, supply chains, wealth management).
