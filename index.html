Quantum · AI

Designing more efficient quantum circuits with Claude

Anthropic · 16 Oct 2026

An AI model iteratively redesigned eight quantum circuit primitives, beating the best published designs on qubit and gate counts.

Our take · DeepTech commentary

If AI can shrink the resources quantum algorithms need, quantum optimization for portfolios and logistics may become practical sooner than expected. This is a core question for our Computational Intelligence track and its links to wealth management.

Quantum

Bringing the magic: Google breaks open a bottleneck in quantum computing

Google · 15 Oct 2026

Google Quantum AI showed “magic state cultivation” on its Willow chip, cutting errors about 40× and reaching 99.99% logical fidelity.

Our take · DeepTech commentary

Lower error rates are a precondition for using quantum hardware on real financial problems. We watch milestones like this to judge when quantum-for-finance research can move from simulation to real machines.

Quantum

ORCA Computing launches PT-3 photonic quantum computer

ORCA Computing · 15 Oct 2026

A photonic quantum system running alongside NVIDIA GPUs in a London data centre, applied to protein design and energy scheduling.

Our take · DeepTech commentary

Hybrid quantum-classical systems running next to GPUs in data centres are the setup most firms will meet first. The energy-scheduling use case speaks directly to our interest in data centres, optimisation and sustainability.

Quantum simulation

Software breakthrough could spur quantum computer advances

EPCC, University of Edinburgh · 15 Oct 2026

A new algorithm runs Gaussian boson sampling simulations with about 5% of the compute previously needed.

Our take · DeepTech commentary

Better classical simulation raises the bar for any claimed quantum advantage. For business researchers it is a reminder to benchmark quantum methods carefully against strong classical baselines before recommending investment.
