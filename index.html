Welcome

DeepTech is a transdisciplinary research cluster at Paris School of Business, uniting Computational Intelligence and Business Intelligence to address problems that significantly affect the global economy.

Our Vision

We see today's most consequential questions at the intersection of technology and business, from how AI reshapes capital allocation to how quantum optimization strengthens supply chains. DeepTech is both a space and a community where researchers, doctoral students and industry partners turn cross-disciplinary ideas into research that matters beyond academia.

⚛

Computational Intelligence

Artificial intelligence, data centres and quantum optimization: the technological substrate of the modern economy.

◎

Business Intelligence

Long-term sustainability, green solutions, resilient supply chains and wealth management.

∞

Quantum for Finance

Where both tracks meet: quantum methods applied to real financial and business problems.

Impact on PSB & Society
