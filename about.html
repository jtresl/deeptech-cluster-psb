About & Mission

DeepTech brings together academics from various backgrounds and schools with the goal to generate wealth that will ultimately benefit PSB and our partners. Given the volatile geopolitical environment, our focus addresses problems that significantly affect the global economy.

A space and a community

Both a physical space and an intellectual community, where researchers, doctoral students, and industry partners converge to exchange ideas, co-develop projects, and turn cross-disciplinary conversations into concrete outputs.

Why it is different

A deliberate pairing of two tracks rarely combined in a business school: the technical frontier of AI, data infrastructure and quantum, and the managerial frontier of sustainability, supply chains and wealth management.

Destination 2030

Aligned with PSB's Destination 2030 strategic plan and its two core pillars: transdisciplinarity and internationalization. The cluster will grow from six founding internal members to at least eight PSB members by the end of Year 2.
