Key papers: quantum & AI in finance

6
  • Quantum computing for finance: overview and prospects
    Orús, Mugel & Lizaso · Reviews in Physics, 2019

    Widely cited early review of quantum methods for optimisation, simulation and ML in finance.

  • Quantum Computing for Finance: State of the Art and Future Prospects
    Egger et al. · IEEE TQE, 2020

    IBM-led overview of quantum algorithms for risk analysis, optimisation and machine learning.

  • A Survey of Quantum Computing for Finance
    Herman et al. · 2022

    Comprehensive survey of quantum algorithms and their finance applications.

  • Option Pricing using Quantum Computers
    Stamatopoulos et al. · Quantum, 2020

    Amplitude-estimation approach to pricing options on quantum hardware.

  • Enhanced fill probability estimates in institutional algorithmic bond trading … with quantum computers
    Ciceri et al. · 2025

    IBM–HSBC study applying hybrid quantum-classical methods to real bond-trading data.

  • Financial Machine Learning
    Kelly & Xiu · NBER Working Paper, 2023

    Survey of machine learning methods in empirical finance and asset pricing.
