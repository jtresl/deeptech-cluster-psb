Activities & Outputs

Research output

Peer-reviewed publications in leading journals across both tracks, with emphasis on joint papers across departments and with external partners.

Events

At least one annual workshop or conference (the annual DeepTech convening, co-organised with a rotating international partner), plus regular internal seminars and writing sessions.

External funding

A pipeline of proposals (Horizon Europe, ERC, ANR, France 2030 / Bpifrance, EIC, industry chairs, foundations), with at least one major proposal per year.

Knowledge mobilization

Engagement with policy makers, industry leaders, NGOs, and media so findings reach audiences beyond academia; quarterly practitioner briefing.

Teaching impact

Research-based teaching content for PSB programs, with particular benefit to the DBA.

Training

Co-supervision of doctoral and DBA projects, a doctoral methods school, masterclasses on AI methods, quantum optimization and sustainability metrics, and mentorship pairings.
